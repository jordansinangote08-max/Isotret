Daily Dose Tracker

A personal tracking tool for recording daily doses and viewing cumulative progress.

How to use it

  1. Open Settings and enter your weight, planned daily dose, target cumulative dose, and optional start date.
  2. Use Log today's dose or the floating + button to save a dose.
  3. Open History to review, edit, or remove saved entries.
  4. Optionally connect JSONBin for cloud backup across devices.

Important

This tracker performs arithmetic based on the values you enter. It does not determine what dose or cumulative target is appropriate for you. Follow the treatment plan given by your qualified healthcare professional.
